## About this repo
<p>First and foremost, this is a test repository that I've created for one singular purpose; entertainment.
A group of me and my friends have recently started playing Dungeons and Dragons over Discord, and I thought it would be fun to make a site for them, detailing their journey, the places they visit, the people they talk to and a little bit of random lore that they uncover. So that's what this site is for. That, and an easy way to navigate infromation, and alternative stories the players never get to see otherwise.</p>
<p><b>Why English?</b><br>
Well, two of the players in question are from the UK, and it felt more natural to write this in the language that the campaign is taking place in.</p>
